## What is Teachable?

[Teachable](https://www.teachable.com/) is an online course creation platform founded in 2013 by [Ankur Nagpal](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ankurnagpal) and Conrad Wadowski, originally under the name Fedora, and acquired by Amsterdam-based Hotmart in March 2020 for a reported $250 million. Together, Hotmart and Teachable serve more than 200,000 creators across nearly every country, with creators on both platforms having earned a combined $10 billion in revenue.

The platform handles what most course creators would otherwise need to assemble from separate tools: video hosting, checkout and payment processing, automatic VAT and sales tax handling across 180+ countries, affiliate management, student mobile apps, and course certificates. That covers the infrastructure stack that would otherwise require a hosted LMS, a payment gateway, a tax compliance tool, and a separate email marketing platform.
