## What is Teachable? Is It Worth It?

Teachable is an online course platform founded in 2013 and headquartered in New York. It powers courses for over 100,000 creators across every category of knowledge — from fitness coaches and language tutors to business consultants, musicians, and finance professionals. Teachable's core value proposition is simplicity: it is designed for non-technical creators who want to build, sell, and deliver online courses without writing code or managing hosting infrastructure.

What Makes Teachable Different

Unlike all-in-one platforms like Kajabi (which includes email marketing, funnels, website building, and membership tools in a single subscription), Teachable is deliberately focused on course creation and delivery. It does not try to replace your email marketing platform, CRM, or sales funnel builder. Instead, it integrates with them — connecting to ConvertKit, Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, Zapier, and hundreds of other tools via native integrations and the API (available on Advanced plan).

The result is a platform that excels at the specific job of creating, hosting, and selling structured educational content — video courses, coaching programmes, digital downloads, and live sessions — with a clean, intuitive interface that most creators are productive in within hours of signing up.

Standout Features of Teachable

Teachable's course builder is genuinely one of the most user-friendly in the market. Content is organised into sections and lectures, with support for video, audio, PDFs, text, quizzes, coding exercises, and downloadable resources — all uploadable with a drag-and-drop interface and no file size limits on video.

Teachable handles payment processing, VAT, and US sales tax automatically on all plans. For international creators, this removes a significant administrative burden — Teachable collects and remits taxes on digital products sold in applicable jurisdictions, handling compliance that would otherwise require separate accounting tools or legal consultation.

The built-in affiliate program (available from the Builder plan) is a standout feature. Creators can recruit affiliates, set custom commission rates, track performance, and manage payouts — all inside Teachable's dashboard without needing a third-party affiliate management tool.

The Teachable mobile app (iOS and Android) is included on all plans and gives students a native mobile learning experience with offline content access, progress tracking, and push notifications.

Is Teachable Worth the Price?

For course creators who are already generating consistent revenue — or who have an established audience to sell to — Teachable is worth it at the Builder tier or above. The platform's reliability, payment automation, affiliate tools, and student management capabilities more than justify the subscription cost once monthly revenue exceeds a few hundred dollars.

The Starter plan is a reasonable starting point for complete beginners who want to test whether online course selling works for their specific niche, but the 7.5% transaction fee makes it economically inferior to Builder as soon as sales volume grows. Creators who start on Starter should have a clear upgrade trigger in mind from the outset.

The platform's main limitations compared to alternatives: it lacks native email marketing (you need a third-party tool), its community features are less developed than dedicated platforms like Circle or Mighty Networks, and its marketing page builder is not as polished as Kajabi's. For creators who need an all-in-one suite, Kajabi or Thinkific may be a better fit. For creators who want the best pure course delivery platform and are comfortable using separate tools for email marketing, Teachable remains one of the top two or three choices in its category in 2026.
